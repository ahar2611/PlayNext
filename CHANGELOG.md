# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-10): first public release

- Ranked **Top 10 / 15 / 25** picks from your own library, for **Overall** and 8 editable categories (Story-rich, RPGs, Action & Combat, Cozy & Chill, Strategy & Tactics, Horror, Short & Sweet, Multiplayer & Co-op).
- One request makes every list plus your **playstyle profile**, so switching categories is instant. Short lists are topped up so you get a full 25 where your library allows it.
- Each pick has a short, plain-language reason and a **New / Continue / Revisit** tag, with **Play**, **Show in library** and **Not interested** buttons.
- **Your playstyle** page: an AI-written summary of how you play, where your hours go, and library stats.
- **HowLongToBeat** story and 100% times. Uses the HowLongToBeat add-on's saved data when installed, otherwise looks them up and caches them. Shows a progress bar for games you've started.
- **AI providers:** Google Gemini (free key, default `gemini-3.6-flash` with automatic backup models), Anthropic Claude, or a built-in offline mode. API keys are stored encrypted with Windows DPAPI.
- Glass-style window whose accent color comes from your top game's artwork, or a color you pick (presets, hex or color wheel).
- **Tray icon** next to the clock (pins itself on Windows 11, can be hidden), plus a Playnite sidebar button and main-menu entries. The sidebar window can be minimized on its own.
